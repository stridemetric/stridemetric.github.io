👋 Welcome to StrideMetric!

To get the most out of the features, load a GPX file for your route.
👉 Export a GPX from OpenRunner, COROS, Garmin or Komoot.
💡 Don't use those apps yet?
Create your route and download a GPX for free at:
👉 calculitineraires.fr
⚡ Quick start
  • 1. Import your routeClick ⊕ GPX or drag your file. Distance, elevation and profile load automatically.
  • 2. Choose your activity🥾 Hiking · 🏃 Running · 🚴 Cycling
  • 3. Set your profileEnter weight, age, gender and heart rate. FTP optional for cycling.
  • 4. Check your zonesCardio Z1→Z5, power/VAM. Stay in the zone suited to your effort.
  • 5. Analyse your outingSpeed, elevation, power, HR, energy — all from your GPX.
  • 6. Optimize your effortZone, duration or max calorie mode. Click ▶ OPTIMIZE.
🆕 Advanced features
  • 💧 HydrationWater target calculated from weight, temperature and duration, with reminders every 20 min.
  • 🍌 NutritionRecommended carb intake per HR zone, with dosing reminders shown on the elevation profile.
  • ⛰️ Climb detectionAutomatic list of climbs (D+, distance, average grade) plus a printable PDF Road Book.
  • ⚡ FTP predictorEstimate your FTP from a climb you have already ridden.
  • 🏁 Race predictorEstimated times for 5K, 10K, half and full marathon based on your threshold pace.
  • ⚠️ Physiological alertsStrideMetric warns you when an effort duration exceeds what is sustainable for the chosen zone.
  • 🪄 OptimizerClick the "Optimize" button to auto-adjust your speeds by zone, target duration, or max-calorie mode, then apply the suggested speeds in one click.
  • 💾 Save / load your profileSave your profile (weight, HR, FTP, zones...) to a file with 💾, and reload it anytime with 📂 — on this device or another.
🎯 Once your GPX is loaded, StrideMetric guides you to adapt your pace and nutrition to your route.
Have a great outing! 🚶‍♂️🚴🏃

🔬 Références scientifiques
Formules et modèles physiologiques/biomécaniques utilisés pour calculer les indicateurs (KPI) de cette application :
  1. Martin, J.C., Milliken, D.L., Cobb, J.E., McFadden, K.L., & Coggan, A.R. (1998). Validation of a mathematical model for road cycling power. Journal of Applied Biomechanics, 14(3), 276–291.
    → modèle de puissance vélo (gravité, aérodynamique, résistance au roulement)
  2. Minetti, A.E., Moia, C., Roi, G.S., Susta, D., & Ferretti, G. (2002). Energy cost of walking and running at extreme uphill and downhill slopes. Journal of Applied Physiology, 93(3), 1039–1046.
    → modèle de coût énergétique course à pied en pente
  3. Pandolf, K.B., Givoni, B., & Goldman, R.F. (1977). Predicting energy expenditure with loads while standing or walking very slowly. Journal of Applied Physiology, 43(4), 577–581.
    → modèle randonnée / marche avec charge portée
  4. Tanaka, H., Monahan, K.D., & Seals, D.R. (2001). Age-predicted maximal heart rate revisited. Journal of the American College of Cardiology, 37(1), 153–156.
    → FC max estimée (hommes)
  5. Gulati, M., Shaw, L.J., Thisted, R.A., Black, H.R., Bairey Merz, C.N., & Arnsdorf, M.F. (2010). Heart rate response to exercise stress testing in asymptomatic women. Circulation, 122(2), 130–137.
    → FC max estimée (femmes)
  6. Karvonen, M.J., Kentala, E., & Mustala, O. (1957). The effects of training on heart rate: a longitudinal study. Annales Medicinae Experimentalis et Biologiae Fenniae, 35(3), 307–315.
    → zones de fréquence cardiaque (méthode de la réserve cardiaque)
  7. Coggan, A.R., & Allen, H. (2010). Training and Racing with a Power Meter (2nd ed.). VeloPress.
    → zones de puissance FTP et seuils de tenue d'effort
  8. Riegel, P.S. (1981). Athletic records and human endurance. American Scientist, 69(3), 285–290.
    → prédicteur de temps de course
  9. Jeukendrup, A.E. (2014). A step towards personalized sports nutrition: carbohydrate intake during exercise. Sports Medicine, 44(Suppl 1), 25–33.
    → apports glucidiques recommandés par zone d'intensité
  10. Sawka, M.N., Burke, L.M., Eichner, E.R., Maughan, R.J., Montain, S.J., & Stachenfeld, N.S. (2007). American College of Sports Medicine position stand: exercise and fluid replacement. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise, 39(2), 377–390.
    → besoins hydriques selon sexe, température et durée
  11. American College of Sports Medicine. (2018). ACSM's Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription (10th ed.). Wolters Kluwer.
    → équations métaboliques standard (VO2, VMA)
  12. Zippenfenig, P. (2023). Open-Meteo.com Weather API [Computer software]. Zenodo. doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7970649
    → prévisions météo horaires (température, ressenti, vent) injectées dans le Road Book horaires, calculées à partir des coordonnées GPS de la trace GPX
  13. Pugh, L.G.C.E. (1971). The influence of wind resistance in running and walking and the mechanical efficiency of work against horizontal or vertical forces. The Journal of Physiology, 213(2), 255–276.
    → traînée aérodynamique du coureur, utilisée pour la vitesse théorique ajustée au vent (vélo & course à pied)
Note : les repères "g/kg/h" et "ml/kg/h" sont des valeurs pratiques usuelles de nutrition/hydratation sportive, à ajuster selon la tolérance individuelle. Ils ne remplacent pas l'avis d'un professionnel de santé.
